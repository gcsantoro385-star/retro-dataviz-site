[2.6] ANALYTICAL INTERPRETATION

RESULTS

These visualizations emphasize comparisons rather than simple frequency counts. Ordering percentages allow groups of different sizes to be compared directly, while the boxplot displays the median, quartiles, spread, and potential age outliers within occupational groups.

The results should be interpreted as associations in this dataset. Differences between demographic or socioeconomic groups do not by themselves demonstrate that those characteristics caused an individual to order food online.

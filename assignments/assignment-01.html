[1.1] RESEARCH PROBLEM & CONTEXT

[STATUS: DOCUMENTED]

Background & Motivation: This dataset consists of information about consumers and their online food ordering behavior. What is also included in this dataset is information about demographic, socioeconomic, educational, family, geographic and customer feedback, which can help identify certain patterns.

> CORE RESEARCH QUESTION

  • RQ1: How does social status determine a person's ordering habits for online food delivery?

> SUB QUESTIONS

  • SQ1: Do online food orders differ based on single and married individuals?
  • SQ2: Which factors have the strongest impact when it comes to ordering food?
  • SQ3: What sort of motivations are involved in these factors for every type of individual?

> TARGET AUDIENCE & STAKEHOLDERS

The visualizations constructed throughout this project are tailored for domain analysts, policy researchers, and academic peers seeking actionable interpretations of complex empirical patterns without sacrificing statistical rigor.

[1.2] VARIABLES & HYPOTHESIS

[STATUS: DOCUMENTED]

> HYPOTHESIS

  • H1: Younger single individuals are more likely to order food online than older married couples.
  • H2: Food ordering habits differ between both married and single individuals depending on factors related to convenience.

> VARIABLES

  • Marital Status
  • Occupation
  • Monthly Income
  • Education
  • Family Size
  • Customer Type
